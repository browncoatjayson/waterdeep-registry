## Mirt's Mansion
**Lot detail:**  Private villa in the foothills of Mount Waterdeep, situated northeast of the moot of Coin Alley and Tarnished Silver Alley. A three-story villa owned by Mirt the Moneylender; also used to conduct business. Has, on occasion, been made available to the Open Lord for use as a private residence outside of the Palace.
