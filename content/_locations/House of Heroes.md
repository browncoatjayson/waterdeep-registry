## House of Heroes
**Lot detail:** Block at Jelzar's Stride and The Lions, in the Jelzar's Stride neighborhood of the Triumphant district of Sea Ward. A three-story temple complex dedicated to Tempus, and containing a museum for the Field of Triumph. Previously destroyed by fire in 1345 DR, and rebuild in 1346 DR.

lot east of the High Street, on the north side of Lamp Street, in the Trades Point North neighborhood of Trades Ward. A one-story dwelling, with shared rental storage space; no basement. Currently the private residence of one Lyskan Hesune, a historian; a business providing sage services is performed on site.
