## Splendon's Residence
**Lot detail:**  Sixth lot east of the High Road, on the north side of The Coffinmarch, in the Spindle Street neighborhood in the Northern Trades Ward district of the Trades Ward. A two-story, class B building, currently the residence of the Splendor family. Previously known as "The Snookery" under owner Myrmith Splendon.
